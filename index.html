01 — Depleted

A pit at the end of its life.

Cleared ground, graded benches, roads already cut and a live grid connection. On the balance sheet it is a closure provision: a cost with no return, held for decades.

02 — Reworked

Restoration and construction, the same job.

Regrading, revegetation, soil carbon baselining and a solar resource assessment. Research presented at UNCCD COP17 shows PV arrays on degraded land actively accelerate ecological recovery rather than compete with it.

03 — Productive

Fifty megawatts on land nobody wanted.

Electricity under a long-term power purchase agreement, carbon credits from grid displacement and land restoration, closure services for the operator, and a lease that pays the district. Five revenue streams stacked on one site.

01 — The premise

Mongolia’s land is 77% degraded. Its renewable pipeline is worth $5.5 billion. These two facts belong together.

Urimor Terra was founded in 2026 by two climate negotiators who kept meeting the same gap: capital ready to move, land ready to be used, and nobody standing in between.

We put ourselves there — selling the panels, writing the finance cases, installing the first systems, and building the software that makes the model repeatable.

02 — Why now

Three forces, one address.

Post-mining restoration duties, the renewable siting crisis and climate finance at scale are converging on the same piece of ground.

01 / Converging force

$100B+

Closure liabilities

Over one hundred billion dollars of mine closure provisions sit on mining balance sheets worldwide. There are 500,000+ abandoned mines in the United States, 50,000+ in Australia, 10,000+ in Canada. In 2027 the Consolidated Mining Standards Initiative ends the era of industry self-assessment.

CMSI 2027IRMAEU Taxonomy

02 / Converging force

3M ha

The siting crisis

Solar PV will occupy roughly three million hectares globally by the early 2030s, up from under 500,000 today. Where to put renewables is becoming as hard a question as whether to build them. Post-mining land arrives with grid connections, cleared surfaces and road access already in place.

Grid-adjacentCleared groundZero land conflict

03 / Converging force

$5.5B

Capital in motion

Mongolia’s renewable pipeline runs to about $5.5 billion over five years. ADB approved a $100M policy loan. EBRD invested $218M in 2025, 53% of it green. The Mongolia Green Finance Corporation launched in August 2026 with $50M in GCF seed funding.
