01 — Depleted

A depleted mine site.

Cleared ground, graded benches, roads already cut and a live grid connection. On the balance sheet it is a closure provision — a cost carried for decades with nothing coming back.

02 — Reworked

A solar array installed.

The same ground, surveyed and built out into rows of photovoltaic panels. Existing access roads and grid infrastructure cut both the capital cost and the time to connection.

03 — Productive

Restored land, still generating.

Vegetation returns to the aisles between the rows while the array keeps producing. Research presented at UNCCD COP17 shows panels on degraded land accelerate ecological recovery rather than compete with it.

About us

A Mongolian renewable energy company, working from supply through to development.

Urimor Terra Development LLC was founded in Ulaanbaatar in 2026 by two climate policy specialists who kept meeting the same gap in the market: capital ready to move, land ready to be used, and no one in between.

Today we supply solar equipment, advise organisations on renewable energy and green finance, and install systems for commercial and community customers. Alongside that we are developing renewable energy projects on depleted mining land, and the software that makes assessing those sites repeatable.

Market context

Why this work matters now.

Mine closure obligations, the difficulty of siting renewables, and climate finance at scale are all arriving on the same piece of ground.

01 / Market driver

$100B+

Mine closure obligations

More than one hundred billion dollars in closure provisions sit on mining company balance sheets worldwide. There are over 500,000 abandoned mines in the United States, 50,000 in Australia and 10,000 in Canada. From 2027 the Consolidated Mining Standards Initiative replaces industry self-assessment with a single externally governed standard.

CMSI 2027IRMAEU Taxonomy

02 / Market driver

3M ha

Where to put renewables

Solar will occupy roughly three million hectares globally by the early 2030s, up from under 500,000 today. Land conflict, permitting and grid access now decide which projects get built. Post-mining land comes with cleared surfaces, road access and grid connections already in place.

Grid-adjacentCleared groundNo land conflict

03 / Market driver

$5.5B

Capital already committed

Mongolia’s renewable pipeline runs to around $5.5 billion over five years. ADB approved a $100 million policy loan. EBRD invested $218 million in 2025, 53% of it green. The Mongolia Green Finance Corporation launched in August 2026 with $50 million in seed funding.

Supporting research

Work from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, presented at UNCCD COP17 in August 2026, shows that solar panels on degraded land actively restore ecosystems: gross primary productivity rose by 110 gC/m² at the Kubuqi Desert, water-use efficiency doubled, and 92% of new Chinese desert solar plants have been built in ecological construction mode since 2017.

Why work with us

How we are different.

01

A validated reference case

The Nalaikh feasibility study demonstrates a 17% pre-tax return on a post-mining site in Mongolia. Our founders contributed to that study, and it is the basis of how we assess sites.

02

Manufacturer relationships

A direct channel to Jinko Solar means competitive pricing, genuine product support and supply certainty for customers who have previously bought through intermediaries.

03

Green finance literacy

We work with the instruments that actually fund projects here — GCF, ADB, EBRD, MGFC, AIIB and Khan Bank — and we know what each one asks for before it will lend.

04

Local origination

We are based in Ulaanbaatar with institutional relationships across GGGI, GIZ, MGFC, ADB and EBRD, in a market that international developers find difficult to enter directly.

05

Aligned incentives

On carbon-linked work we take a share of the revenue rather than a fixed fee, so we are paid when the project performs.
