Mine land solar

Closed mines make good solar sites.

A closed mine has cleared ground, access roads and a grid connection already on site. That makes it cheaper and faster to build on than open land.

We assess closed and depleted mine sites in Mongolia for solar and storage, and we are building software that takes site data and produces closure and reuse options.

Our founders contributed to a feasibility study for a 50 MW solar and storage project at Nalaikh, Mongolia’s largest closed mine site. Urimor Terra does not own or operate that project.
