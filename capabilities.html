Sequencing

Earn the right to build the next one.

Four business lines, ordered by capital intensity and team readiness rather than by ambition.

Lines one and two generate near-term revenue and build the commercial record. Line three proves we can execute a physical project. Line four — utility-scale infrastructure and the software platform — is the scaling play that becomes financeable only once the first three have produced a track record.

Distribution and consulting pay for the company. Installation proves it can deliver. Infrastructure and software are what those first three earn us the right to build.

01 — Revenue line

Solar panel distribution

Jinko Solar channel partner, Mongolia

Live

We distribute Jinko Solar modules and components in Mongolia, acting as the in-country sales, logistics and after-sales channel for mining operations, industrial facilities, commercial buildings and residential customers.

Why this, now

Jinko Solar is the world’s largest solar module manufacturer, currently in global oversupply. A business development trip to Shanghai in June 2026 established the relationship directly. Mongolian policy is wary of direct Chinese equity in energy — but buying Chinese-manufactured panels through a Mongolian distributor is standard and uncontroversial. Urimor provides that channel.

How it earns

Distributor margin

8–15% on module sales

Wholesale price band

$0.25–$0.30 per watt

Typical project size

10 kW – 1 MW

Year 1 target

200 kW – 1 MW of panel sales

What it really is

Distribution margins are thin and the Mongolian solar market is small. This line will not make the company wealthy. It generates cash flow from month one, builds installer and customer relationships, and gives Urimor a legitimate commercial presence in the solar value chain. It is the credibility-builder that makes everything else possible.

02 — Revenue line

Consulting & green finance

Advisory for organisations navigating the transition

Live

Renewable energy advisory, ESG consulting, green finance navigation and mine closure planning for Mongolian and international organisations.

Why this, now

This is the line that best leverages what the founding team actually has: multilateral networks, policy knowledge and green finance literacy built across UNFCCC, UNICEF GenU, GGGI, GIZ and the Mongolian DFI ecosystem.

How it earns

Engagement fee

$2,000 – $15,000 per project

Year 1 target

5–10 engagements

Gross margin

Approx. 80%

Delivery

Founder-led, 2–8 weeks

What it really is

Four service lines sit inside this: green finance readiness assessments against GCF, ADB, EBRD, MGFC and AIIB / Khan Bank instruments; renewable energy pre-feasibility scoping using the Nalaikh methodology as a reference framework; ESG and sustainability advisory against CMSI 2027, IRMA and EU Taxonomy alignment; and carbon credit project scoping under Verra VM0047 and VM0042.

03 — Revenue line

Small-scale solar installation

5 kW to 100 kW, delivered turnkey

First project in progress

Design, supply and installation of small-scale solar systems for commercial buildings, ger districts, mining camp facilities and community centres — combining our Jinko distribution with turnkey delivery through licensed local electrical contractors.

Why this, now

Installation is where a solar company stops being a trader and starts being an operator. The first demonstration system — 5 to 20 kW on a community building or partner site — is the smallest credible proof that we can deliver a real project.

How it earns

Turnkey price

$1.50 – $2.50 per watt installed

Typical 20 kW system

$30,000 – $50,000 project value

Gross margin

15–25%

Year 1 target

2 completed projects

What it really is

We do not self-install. Every system is delivered with licensed Mongolian electrical contractors, because one bad installation would undo the credibility the other lines are built to earn. Mongolia’s winters compress the installation season, so scheduling is planned around it rather than against it.

04 — Revenue line

Post-mining infrastructure & the Mine-to-Value platform

The long horizon the first three lines pay for

Demo stage

Utility-scale solar plus storage on depleted mining sites, and an AI-powered SaaS platform that turns mine site data into optimised closure plans, renewable feasibility analysis, carbon credit projections, regulatory compliance dashboards and investor-grade financial models.

Why this, now

Every incumbent treats mine closure as a cost to manage. Nobody treats it as a value creation opportunity to optimise. We are building the infrastructure and the software at the same time, which means the platform’s outputs are checked against real project economics rather than against themselves.

How it earns

PPA electricity (50 MW)

~$9.5M per year

Carbon credits

~$900K per year

SaaS subscription

$50K – $150K per site, per year

Feasibility-as-a-service

$25K – $75K per assessment

What it really is

This line becomes actionable once Urimor has twelve months of operating history with revenue, at least one completed installation, a working software demo and warm DFI relationships. Realistically that is 2027 for the software and 2028 at the earliest for infrastructure. It is stated here as a direction, not a promise.
