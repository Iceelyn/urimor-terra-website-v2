01 — At a glance

Who we are.

Urimor Terra Development LLC is a Mongolian climate-infrastructure venture.

We distribute solar panels as a Jinko Solar channel partner, provide renewable energy and ESG consulting, install small-scale solar systems, and are building toward utility-scale renewable energy on depleted mining sites — alongside AI-powered software designed to scale that model beyond Mongolia.

We sit at the intersection of Mongolia’s $5.5 billion renewable energy investment pipeline, more than $100 billion in global mine closure liabilities, and rising demand for climate finance advisory in emerging markets.

Urimor Terra is the founding and only operating entity of a planned three-entity group. Closure OS (AI and technology) and Empyra (venture capital) are strategic intent, not registered companies. Everything on this site describes Urimor Terra standalone.

02 — Mission & vision

What we are for.

Mission

To accelerate Mongolia’s transition to renewable energy by making solar accessible through distribution, helping organisations navigate green finance through consulting, and transforming depleted mining sites into productive renewable energy assets — while building the technology that lets mining companies elsewhere convert closure liabilities into revenue-generating infrastructure.

Vision

To become Mongolia’s leading solar distribution and green energy advisory company, with a credible long-term path toward developing post-mining renewable energy infrastructure at scale — and the platform that developers, mining companies and development finance institutions use to plan, finance and execute mine-to-solar transitions.

We have deliberately written both of those to describe what this company can actually become within two to three years. An earlier version of our plan claimed rather more. It read well and it was not true.

What this team is, and is not

What it is: two highly networked, policy-literate founders with genuine access to multilateral institutions and a working relationship with the world’s largest solar manufacturer. What it is not, yet: project developers, electrical engineers, or operators with a long commercial track record. The plan is built around acquiring those capabilities in sequence — through partners, subcontractors, accelerators and eventually hires — rather than around pretending they already exist.

06 — What we’re looking for

Small, specific and sequenced.

A raise a two-person pre-revenue company can credibly close, spent on things that produce evidence. Earn the right to raise more by delivering results with less.

Phase 0 · Now

Bootstrap launch

$15,000 – $30,000

Initial Jinko Solar inventory and first customer order deposit, a 5–20 kW demonstration installation on a community or partner site, software demo development, consulting launch costs, and a small working capital buffer.

Sources — co-founder savings, small grants, angel investment

Phase 1 · After 6–12 months of revenue

Growth capital

$50,000 – $100,000

Inventory expansion toward 500 kW–1 MW of annual distribution capacity, installation equipment, software MVP development beyond demo stage, and Australian entity registration for market entry.

Sources — revenue reinvestment, accelerator investment, innovation grants

Phase 2+ · Not yet

Infrastructure development

Blended DFI finance

Utility-scale project development and construction financing through ADB, EBRD, GCF, MGFC and commercial banks. This becomes a real conversation once there is eighteen months of operating history, a portfolio of completed installations, warm DFI relationships and a signed memorandum with a mining company partner.

The financing architecture exists and is proven. The question is whether we earn access to it.
