About us

A solar company in Ulaanbaatar.

Urimor Terra Development LLC was registered in Mongolia in 2026. Two co-founders run it full time.

We sell solar equipment, install systems from 5 to 100 kW, and advise organisations on renewable energy projects and how to fund them. We also develop solar and storage on closed mine land, and we are building software to assess those sites. That part of the business is not yet trading.

We are a young company. Where we have not done something yet, this site says so.

Mission and vision

What we are here to do.

Mission

We supply and install solar power for businesses and communities across Mongolia.

Vision

Every closed mine in Mongolia generates power for the people living near it.

Track record

How we got here.

The company was registered in 2026. The work behind it started three years earlier.

  1. 2023

    First multilateral appointments

    • Both founders join UNYAP Mongolia, the UN Youth Advisory Panel.
    • Naranzaya is selected as a UNFCCC COP28 youth negotiator through the Climate Youth Negotiator Programme.
    • Anu attends the Student Energy Summit in the UAE.

  2. 2024

    Published research and policy roles

    • Both contribute to a Columbia SIPA report on advancing Mongolia’s transition to renewable energy.
    • Anu is selected as an East Asian YPAT member.
    • Naranzaya is selected as a Global YPAT member.

  3. 2025

    International platforms

    • Pitched at Civil Society Week in Bangkok.
    • Finalists in Hack the Future: Young Leaders for Asia.
    • Naranzaya joins the UNICEF Generation Unlimited Global Leadership Council.

  4. 2026

    Company founded and trading

    • Urimor Terra Development LLC is registered in Mongolia and both founders move to it full time.
    • Completed the AI Ventures Accelerator, run by Technovation with Generation Unlimited.
    • Contributed to the Nalaikh 50 MW solar-plus-storage feasibility study.
    • Attended UNCCD COP17 in Ulaanbaatar.
    • Business development visit to Shanghai to establish module supply.
