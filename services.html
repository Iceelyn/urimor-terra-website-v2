How the services fit together

Supply, advise, install, develop.

Four service lines, ordered by how much each asks of us as a company.

Supply and consulting are open for business today and are how most clients first work with us. Installation extends that into delivery, so a customer can buy equipment and have it designed, fitted and commissioned by the same company.

Post-mining energy development is the longer horizon, and it is the reason the company exists. We build the first three properly so that the fourth is credible when we come to it.

01 — Service

Solar panel supply

Jinko Solar channel partner, Mongolia

Available now

We supply Jinko Solar modules and components across Mongolia, acting as the in-country sales, logistics and after-sales channel for mining operations, industrial facilities, commercial buildings and residential customers.

Why customers come to us

Jinko Solar is the world’s largest solar module manufacturer. A business development visit to Shanghai in June 2026 established the relationship directly, which means our customers buy manufacturer-supported product through a Mongolian company, with local warranty handling and local accountability.

At a glance

Products

Modules, inverters and balance of system

Order sizes

10 kW to 1 MW

Customers

Mining, industrial, commercial, residential

Support

In-country logistics, warranty and after-sales

How we work

The Mongolian solar market is still small and fragmented, and most existing suppliers are traders without a manufacturer relationship or technical support capability. Supply is where we start with most customers, and it is often what leads to the design and installation work that follows.

02 — Service

Consulting and green finance

Advisory for organisations navigating the transition

Available now

Renewable energy advisory, ESG consulting, green finance navigation and mine closure planning for Mongolian and international organisations.

Why customers come to us

Money for renewable projects in Mongolia is available, but it comes through instruments with specific requirements. We work with those instruments day to day and can tell a client early which ones they qualify for and what each will ask them to produce.

At a glance

Green finance readiness

ESG advisory

Alignment with CMSI 2027, IRMA and the EU Taxonomy

Carbon scoping

Verra VM0047 and VM0042 eligibility and revenue potential

How we work

Engagements typically run from two to eight weeks and are led directly by the founders. We draw on working knowledge of the Green Climate Fund, ADB, EBRD, the Mongolia Green Finance Corporation, and the AIIB facility at Khan Bank.

03 — Service

Solar installation

5 kW to 100 kW, delivered turnkey

First projects underway

Design, supply and installation of small-scale solar systems for commercial buildings, ger districts, mining camp facilities and community centres, combining our own supply with turnkey delivery through licensed local electrical contractors.

Why customers come to us

Many buildings in Mongolia that would benefit from solar are not large enough to interest a utility-scale developer and not simple enough for an off-the-shelf kit. That range — a commercial roof, a community building, a mining camp — is where we work.

At a glance

System size

5 kW to 100 kW

Scope

Design, supply, installation, commissioning

Delivery

With licensed Mongolian electrical contractors

Season

Installation planned around the Mongolian winter

How we work

We do not self-install. Every system is delivered with licensed local contractors, and we stay responsible for design, supply and commissioning. Quality matters more than volume here: one poorly executed installation would undo the reputation the rest of the business depends on.

04 — Service

Post-mining energy projects

Renewable development on depleted mining land

In development

Renewable energy projects on depleted mining sites, supported by software that turns mine closure data into closure plans, renewable feasibility analysis, carbon credit projections and investment-grade financial models.

Why customers come to us

Mine closure is normally treated as a cost to be managed. A depleted site with cleared ground, road access and a live grid connection is also one of the better places to put a renewable project — and doing both at once turns a liability into an asset for the operator, the district and the grid.

At a glance

Site types

Depleted and closed mining land

Technology

Solar with battery storage

Analysis

Feasibility, closure planning, carbon assessment

Status

In development; software at demonstration stage

How we work

This is a longer-horizon line of work and we describe it as such. It becomes deliverable once we have a longer operating history, completed installations, a working software product and established relationships with development finance institutions. We would rather set that out plainly than imply a capability we do not yet have.
