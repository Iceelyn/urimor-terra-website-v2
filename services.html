01

Solar equipment supply

Modules, inverters and mounting systems

Available now

We supply solar modules, inverters, mounting systems and cabling from tier-1 manufacturers to mining operations, industrial sites, commercial buildings and homes across Mongolia.

Products

Modules, inverters, mounting, cabling

Order size

10 kW to 1 MW

Included

Import logistics and customs clearance

After sale

Warranty handling and technical support

Most solar equipment in Mongolia is sold by traders who cannot support the product after delivery. We handle the import, the paperwork and the warranty claim, and we are in the country if something goes wrong.

02

Consulting

Renewable energy and green finance advisory

Available now

We advise organisations planning renewable energy projects in Mongolia, and organisations that need to work out how to pay for them.

Green finance

Which funders and programmes a project can apply to

Feasibility

Early-stage assessment before a project is committed

ESG

Reporting and standards work for mining and industrial clients

Closure planning

Reuse options for mine sites at end of life

Work is delivered by the founders. A typical engagement runs two to eight weeks and ends with a document a client can put in front of a board or a lender.

03

Solar installation

5 to 100 kW, designed and commissioned

Available now

We design, supply and install solar systems for commercial buildings, ger districts, mining camp facilities and community buildings.

System size

5 kW to 100 kW

Scope

Design, supply, installation, commissioning

Electrical work

Licensed Mongolian contractors

Scheduling

Planned around the winter season

We do not carry out the electrical work ourselves. It is done by licensed Mongolian contractors, and we stay responsible for design, supply and commissioning.

Mine land solar

In development

Sites

Technology

Software

Demonstration stage

Status
